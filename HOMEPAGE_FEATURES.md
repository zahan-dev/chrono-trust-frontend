# ChronoTrust Luxury Homepage

## Overview
A premium, modern luxury homepage for ChronoTrust - a family-owned luxury watch business focused on trust, transparency, and expertise.

## Design Style
- **Clean, minimal, high-end luxury aesthetic**
- Inspired by premium brands like Rolex
- Focus on whitespace, typography, and visual hierarchy
- Mobile-first responsive design

## Color Theme
- **Primary:** Blue (#3B82F6)
- **Background:** White (#FFFFFF)
- **Accent:** Slate/Gray tones
- Subtle gradients and soft shadows

## Typography
- **Primary Font:** Inter (modern sans-serif)
- **Accent Font:** Playfair Display (luxury serif)
- Strong hierarchy with large headings
